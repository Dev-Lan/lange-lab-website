# What we mean by visualization research

June 2, 2026 — Devin Lange

_This is a placeholder post._

"Visualization research" covers a wide range of work, so it is worth saying what
we mean by it here. Our projects usually start with a collaborator who has data
they cannot make sense of with the tools they have, and end with a system plus
some understanding of whether it helped.

That middle part — designing the thing, building it, and then honestly
evaluating it — is where most of the interesting problems live.
