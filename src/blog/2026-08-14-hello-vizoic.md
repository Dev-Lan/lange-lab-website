## h2 2

Vizoic Lab is up and running, and this is where we will post about what we are
working on. Expect a mix of project updates, notes from conferences, and
occasional longer write-ups about visualization design decisions that we found
interesting enough to explain.

Posts live as markdown files in `src/blog/`. Adding one is a single new file —
the [blog index](/blog/) picks it up automatically and sorts by the `date` field
in the frontmatter.
