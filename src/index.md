# Vizoic Lab

Vizoic Lab studies how interactive data visualization can help people make sense
of complex scientific data. We build visualization systems and study how they are
used, with a focus on biomedical data — single-cell microscopy, genomics, and the
messy tabular datasets that underpin published results.

We work across the whole arc of a visualization project: understanding a domain
problem alongside collaborators, designing and building a tool, and evaluating
whether it actually changed how the work gets done.
